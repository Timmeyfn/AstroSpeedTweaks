Making the AstroSpeed installer (the "next, next, finish" setup)
================================================================

This turns the single exe into AstroSpeed-Setup.exe: a normal installer that
puts AstroSpeed in Program Files, adds a Start Menu shortcut (and an optional
desktop shortcut), and gives people a proper Uninstall entry.

ONE-TIME
1. Download Inno Setup (free): https://jrsoftware.org/isdl.php  (the "isetup" exe)
2. Install it.

EACH TIME YOU HAVE A NEW BUILD
1. Build the app so you have "AstroSpeed Tweaks.exe" (run build.bat).
2. Copy "AstroSpeed Tweaks.exe" into this "installer" folder, next to AstroSpeed.iss.
3. Double-click AstroSpeed.iss. Inno Setup opens.
4. Press the Build menu > Compile  (or the green play button).
5. AstroSpeed-Setup.exe appears in this folder. That's the installer you share.
   (When you release a new version, change AppVersion at the top of AstroSpeed.iss.)


ABOUT THE "WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC" MESSAGE
---------------------------------------------
An installer does NOT make this go away, and neither does adding an antivirus
exception - here is what's really happening and what actually helps.

The warning is SmartScreen. Windows shows it for any program that isn't yet
widely downloaded AND isn't signed with a paid code-signing certificate. The
app isn't doing anything wrong; it just hasn't built up "reputation" yet.

What does NOT help, and what to avoid:
  - Telling people to turn off Windows Security or add an exclusion. That's
    exactly what real viruses ask for, so it makes people trust it less, and
    it leaves their PC unprotected. Don't do it.

What actually helps:
  - Tell people: on the blue warning, click "More info" then "Run anyway".
    That is normal for small indie tools and is all most people need.
  - The warning fades on its own as more people download the same exact file.
  - The real fix is a code-signing certificate (about US$100-200/year from
    sellers like Sectigo or DigiCert). Sign the exe and the installer with it
    and the warning goes away. An EV certificate removes it immediately.
  - If Defender ever deletes/quarantines the exe (not just warns), it's a
    false positive. Submit the file at
    https://www.microsoft.com/wdsi/filesubmission  and it usually clears in a
    day or two for everyone.
