Releasing an update
===================
1. Build the new exe (run build.bat; remember to raise AppVersion in main.go first).
2. Open https://astrospeed.dupelication.workers.dev/admin > Release tab.
3. Pick the new "AstroSpeed Tweaks.exe". The version fills in by itself.
4. Type what's new > Upload and publish.

Everyone with the app gets "Update now" next time they open it, and it installs
inside the app with a progress bar. The website download switches to the new file.
